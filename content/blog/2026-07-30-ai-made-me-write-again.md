More than four years have elapsed since my last blog post. I'm starting this up again. At the very least, I'm writing this now and I have a couple of ideas for some more posts I want to write. We'll see how long the inspiration lasts.

I have been inspired to write again by AI. By bad AI writing, to be exact. You know the sort. I don't even need to elaborate at this stage.

I'm by no means anti-AI. I use it constantly at work. It has transformed what I am capable of getting done as a software developer. I'm not anti-AI; I am pro-human.

I like people and I'm interested in their ideas. For that reason I really hate AI writing. I hate to read an article or social media post and be left with no idea about whether the author actually shared an idea. Maybe the article did start with their own idea which they fed to the AI to "make it sound good". But on the other hand maybe they just prompted the AI to "write me a blog post about something that will get engagement". I have no idea either way.
